# Manager user guide

Managers run catalog, stock, checkout, returns, orders and operational reports but cannot administer owner-only employee settings.

## Stock and suppliers

Create products with unique SKUs, integer LKR prices, correct tax and stock-tracking settings. Record every receipt, correction or adjustment with a meaningful reference/reason. Never change stock to conceal a checkout discrepancy. Review low-stock alerts and supplier details.

## Sales and adjustments

Confirm customer, quantities, discounts and tax before checkout. Finalized invoices are historical records. Record partial or mixed payments against the invoice; a deposit is payment received, not extra sales. For a return, select original lines/quantities, state whether stock is resalable, approve the resulting credit, then refund no more than the available credit.

## Optical orders

Link the customer and finalized examination, optionally the invoice. Maintain supplier reference and progress statuses. Complete all QC checks before ready-for-collection. Use an outstanding-balance collection override only with explicit business authorization and notes.

Reconcile Reports at close and notify the owner of overrides, refunds, large discounts and stock adjustments.
